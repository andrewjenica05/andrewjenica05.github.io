Rocket League Training

FREEPLAY

1. Watch Pros on YouTube

a. Tournaments
b. Arsenal
c. SunlessKhan
d. Different Matches

2. Hit the ball as hard as you can; chase after it

a. Gives you many situations to be in and warms you up; work on speed and mechanics
b. BIG WIDE TURNS TO HIT THE BALL, no micro adjustments
c. Practice more drifts
   i. Tap the drift instead of holding; brake more

3. Recovering

a. Hold powerslide always when landing
b. Point nose directly down on a wall when recovering
c. Land quickly with all 4 wheels on the ground, correct orientation/facing the direction you are about to travel, maintain speed
d. Land fast
   i. If you are not going for a specific hit, go to the ground as soon as possible

4. Kickoff flip

a. PRACTICE DIFFERENT KICKOFFS
b. Change direction to the ball slightly, lightly tap boost, do one forward flip, boost again, go one more forward flip right before you touch the ball

5. Study the minipads

a. Minipads rotations

6. Dribbles and flicks

a. Put the ball on the roof of the ground; dribble while the ball is off the ground

7. Learn how to aim your car in the air

a. Push yourself as early as possible; push out of your comfort zone

8. Learn wall play

a. Leave the ball completely. Flip off the wall in different directions, learn how your car moves on the wall, and understand the change of perspective
b. Use the back wall to defend properly

9. Practice driving backwards

a. Go into free play and drive backwards to control better

10. Half Flips

a. Practice in free play constantly; use in-game constantly

11. Training Packs - Practice the fundamentals

a. Wall plays, dribbles, power shots, accuracy
b. Learn new mechanics at my ability
c. Don't worry about training more mechanics

IN GAME

12. Focus on the Basics

a. Minimize hesitation
   i. Go for the ball faster and miss than go slow and have no chance at all
   ii. BOOST CONFIDENCE
   iii. PRIORITIZE BALL OVER BOOST
b. Basic Powershot
   i. Timing of your flip when you take power shots is most important
      1. Flip at the exact moment just BEFORE your touch to get the most power on it
   ii. Wait for the ball just after it bounces, then flip into it
      1. FRONT FLIP IS MORE POWERFUL
   iii. Focus on aiming the ball
      1. Power drift to be more accurate in hitting the ball in the right direction/spot and still keep the momentum of the ball
   iv. Hit the ball with more power with more powerful turns
   v. Always try to hit the ball straight on
      1. FRONT FLIPS ONLY
      2. Position the ball better so the ball is always between the destination
c. Strong clears
   i. Learn more power clears. When the ball goes fast and hard to the opponent's side
      1. When the ball is rolling flat on the ground, don't jump into it or flip right into the middle.
d. Keeping momentum up
   i. Recoveries
e. Basic Aerials
   i. Avoid the walls
      1. Focus on predicting where the ball is going to land off the wall and in the air
   ii. REDUCE RANDOM JUMPS
      1. Do not leave the ground without a specific purpose
f. Don't double commit
   i. Don’t follow behind teammates
g. Space out from teammates
   i. Filling threatening gaps that your opponents are taking over
   ii. Filling as much ground as possible
h. Far post rotations
   i. The quickest path is not the best path
      1. Rotate instead of going for a quick shot on goal; plan your path
   ii. If you are not confident in hitting a shot, just rotate to get in a better position
i. Far post rotations
   i. NO OUTPLAYING OPPONENTS
   ii. Waiting for them to mess up, then punish. Play more defense
   iii. Drive back to the net and wait for the opponent to take the shot, then counterattack.
   iv. Don't always rush; have patience
j. Predict where the ball will travel

13. Advanced Playstyles

a. Airroll
b. WALL PLAYS!!
c. Learning Aerials
d. Recognize Playstyles
e. Dribbling and Flicking
f. Watch and Predict Plays
g. Don't Play Fast
   i. Conserve your boost
      1. Do you need to boost?
      2. Can you collect more boost on the way?
      3. Will boosting put me in the best position?
   ii. HAVE MORE PATIENCE
      1. Wait for the ball to return to the ground so you can do an easy flip; don’t go for aerials

Rocket League is about scoring more goals than your opponent
